Step 1 of 5 — Research Phase
Find what your audience is already searching for
Before you build a single post, run these prompts in Claude or ChatGPT. Your campaign is only as strong as your research. Copy each prompt, fill in your details, and save the results.
Prompt 01
Uncover what your buyer is secretly afraid of
You are a consumer psychology researcher. My ideal customer is [describe your audience] and I sell [your product or service]. I need to understand the real fear underneath their buying hesitation — not the excuse they give, but the deeper emotion driving it. Search Amazon reviews, Facebook group posts, and Reddit threads where people in this audience talk about products like mine. Find the 3 most repeated emotional fears. Write them in their exact words, not polished language.
Prompt 02
Find out what makes your audience finally say yes
You are a buying behavior analyst. My audience is [describe your audience] and my Black Friday offer is [your offer]. I need to know what specific trigger pushes someone in this audience from interested to ready to buy. Search YouTube comments, Etsy reviews, and online community posts where people describe the moment they decided to purchase something similar. What did they say convinced them? Give me the top 3 decision triggers in their own language.
Prompt 03
Identify what your competition is getting wrong
You are a competitive intelligence analyst. My niche is [your niche] and my audience is [describe your audience]. Search for the top 5 creators or sellers in my space and find the most common complaints people have about their content, products, or communication style. Look in comment sections, review platforms, and community forums. Give me 3 specific gaps or frustrations my audience has that competitors are not solving. This is where my campaign should position me differently.
Prompt 04
Find the words that make your audience stop scrolling
You are a copywriting researcher. My audience is [describe your audience] and they spend time on [your platforms]. I need to find the exact phrases, words, and sentence patterns that make this audience stop scrolling and engage. Search high-performing posts in my niche from the last 90 days. Look at what the first line says in posts with the most comments and shares. Give me 5 opening line patterns with examples, and explain why each one works for this specific audience.
Prompt 05
Discover what your audience tried before that failed them
You are a market research specialist. My audience is [describe your audience] and they are looking for [the result your offer delivers]. Before finding a solution like mine, they likely tried other things that did not work. Search online communities, comment sections, and review threads where this audience talks about previous attempts and failures. Find the top 3 things they tried that disappointed them and the exact language they used to describe why it did not work. I will use this in my campaign to show them I understand their journey.
Prompt 06
Map your audience's Black Friday buying mindset
You are a seasonal consumer behavior analyst. My audience is [describe your audience] and I am planning a Black Friday campaign for [your offer]. I need to understand how this specific audience thinks and behaves during Black Friday. Search for posts, threads, and discussions where people like my audience talk about Black Friday shopping decisions. What makes them buy during this season versus wait? What offers excite them versus feel like a gimmick? Give me 3 insights I can use to position my campaign so it feels like exactly what they were waiting for.
