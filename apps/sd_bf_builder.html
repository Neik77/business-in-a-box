Step 1 of 5 — Research Phase
Find what your audience is already searching for
Before you build a single post, run these prompts in Claude or ChatGPT. Your campaign is only as strong as your research. Copy each prompt, fill in your details, and save the results.
Prompt 01
Find what your scattered message is costing you
Act as a research team. I am a [your profession] with expertise in [your area]. Help me understand what happens when my messaging is scattered, and give me 3 things I am losing right now by jumping between topics. Search Reddit and YouTube for posts where people say they do not follow a creator because they cannot figure out what that creator does. Show me the exact frustration they expressed.
Prompt 02
Find what your audience is searching for right now
Act as a research team. My audience is [describe your specific audience]. Help me find what they are searching for right now and give me 3 specific messages I could own. Search Google Trends and Reddit for the top 10 questions my audience is asking this month. Show me what they are desperate to know. Show me gaps where nobody is answering clearly.
Prompt 03
Find the language your audience actually uses
Act as a research team. My audience is [describe your audience]. Show me exactly how they talk about their problems and give me 3 specific phrases I can use in my content. Find 10 posts from creators my audience actually follows. What language do they use? What tone wins? Show me their real words.
Prompt 04
Find the gap nobody in your niche is filling
Act as a research team. My audience is [describe your audience]. Help me find what content gaps exist right now and give me 3 specific ideas based on what people are actually searching for. Search for topics with real search volume but poor answers. What are people asking that is not being answered well?
